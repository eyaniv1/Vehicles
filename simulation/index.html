Logic Gates

GateEffect
DirectNo filtering — raw sensor values pass straight through
ThresholdSigmoid activation — ignores dim light below the threshold, reacts strongly above it
ANDBoth sensors must detect light above threshold before the vehicle reacts — responds only to centered, strong light
OREither sensor detecting light above threshold triggers a reaction — responds to light from any direction
XORReacts when sensors differ (one bright, one dim) — follows the edges and boundaries of light fields

Each gate has two parameters: Threshold (0–80) sets the activation point, and Sharpness (1–20) controls how abruptly the gate switches on.

Presets

PresetApplies ToDescription
Social ModeAll vehiclesTurns on mounted lights (50%), enables side sensors with Fear behavior at 25% blend. Creates mutual awareness — vehicles see each other's lights and react laterally.
Focused HunterSelectedV2b Aggression + AND gate (T:25, S:8). Only charges at strong, centered light. Ignores dim or off-center sources.
Edge FollowerSelectedV2b Aggression + XOR gate (T:15, S:6). Follows the boundary between light and dark rather than heading straight for the source.
Cautious SocialSelectedV2b Aggression + OR front gate + Side Fear with AND gate. Approaches any visible light but shies away laterally when too close to others.
SwarmSelectedV3a Love + Threshold gates + light on + Side Aggression. Gathers near light sources in groups, with lateral aggression creating swirling flock behavior.

Selected Vehicle Readout

The six values at the bottom of the control panel show real-time data for the currently selected vehicle:

ValueMeaning
Left EyeLight intensity detected by the left front sensor (0–100). Higher = brighter light on the left side.
Right EyeLight intensity detected by the right front sensor (0–100). Higher = brighter light on the right side.
Side L / Side RLight intensity at the left and right lateral sensors (shown only when side sensors are enabled).
Left MotorCurrent speed of the left wheel (0–100). Driven by sensor input through wiring and gates.
Right MotorCurrent speed of the right wheel (0–100). The difference between left and right motor speeds determines turning.

When both motors are equal the vehicle goes straight. When they differ, it turns toward the slower wheel.

Vehicle Card Settings

Start/Stop — each vehicle runs independently.
Light — mounted light visible to other vehicles, creating social interactions.
Speed — simulation speed multiplier (1x–5x).
Contrast — amplifies motor speed differences for sharper turns.
F.Gate / S.Gate — logic gate on front / side sensor pair.
Side — enable lateral sensors with independent behavior type.
Blend — mix ratio between front and side motor output.
Explain — generates a plain-English description of expected behavior.

World

The world is a torus — vehicles wrap around edges and sensors detect light across boundaries (shortest toroidal distance). Choose world size from the dropdown. Zooming out reveals more of the world.

How a Braitenberg Vehicle Works

Each vehicle has light sensors connected to wheel motors. The wiring determines behavior:

Direct wiring — left sensor drives left motor, right sensor drives right motor:

When light is brighter on the left, the left motor speeds up, turning the vehicle away from the light (Fear). With inhibitory response, brighter light slows the motor, so the vehicle turns toward the light and stops near it (Love).

Crossed wiring — left sensor drives right motor, right sensor drives left motor:

When light is brighter on the left, the right motor speeds up, steering the vehicle toward the light (Aggression). With inhibitory response, the vehicle approaches but veers away up close (Explorer).

Gates let you create complex behaviors: an AND gate makes a vehicle ignore peripheral light; an XOR gate makes it follow light boundaries instead of heading to the center.
